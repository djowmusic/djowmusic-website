About

The story

From a small pub to the big stage

  1. 2013 · Lebanon

    It started behind the decks of a small pub in Lebanon, where DJOW played as the resident DJ.

  2. 2022 · Dubai

    He moved to Dubai. The city put him in front of crowds from every corner of the Arab world, and he built a sound for all of them: Khaleeji rhythm, Oriental melody and Afro House drums in one set.

  3. Along the way

    50,000 people at Expo 2020 Dubai. 30,000 at the FIFA World Cup Qatar 2022 fan festival. MDLBEAST, Mawsem Al Riyadh and 14 US cities on tour with DJ Aseel.

  4. Today

    Resident at Mood Rooftop and EPIK. Thirteen years in, he also builds for the next DJ: the DJOW RVRS and DJOW SGNL plugins, the free Sharq sample packs and OWVRSZ, the premium oversized apparel label he wears on stage.

A DJOW night should be memorable. Every song sung back, a throwback you didn’t see coming, and a memory you take home.

DJOW is an international open format DJ and producer, born in Lebanon and based in Dubai.

He started DJing in 2013. Today his sets move from Khaleeji to Oriental to Afro House to commercial without losing the floor, and his remixes give Arabic classics a club pulse. His Fairuz Afro remix of Btetloj El Deni is one of them.

He is resident at Mood Rooftop and EPIK in Dubai and plays across the Gulf, Lebanon, Egypt and Europe. He has played to 50,000+ at Expo 2020 Dubai, 30,000+ at the FIFA World Cup Qatar 2022 fan festival, and at MDLBEAST and Mawsem Al Riyadh. In 2022 and 2023 he toured 14 US cities with DJ Aseel.

Off stage he builds tools for other producers, including the DJOW RVRS reverse reverb plugin, the free DJOW SGNL metering plugin and the Sharq sample packs. He also runs OWVRSZ, a premium oversized apparel label.

Speaks Arabic, English and French.
