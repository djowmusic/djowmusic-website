About

DJOW is an international open format DJ and producer, born in Lebanon and based in Dubai.

He started DJing in 2013. Today his sets move from Khaleeji to Oriental to Afro House to commercial without losing the floor, and his remixes give Arabic classics a club pulse. His Fairuz Afro remix of Btetloj El Deni is one of them.

He is resident at Mood Rooftop and EPIK in Dubai and plays across the Gulf, Lebanon, Egypt and Europe. He has played to 50,000+ at Expo 2020 Dubai, 30,000+ at the FIFA World Cup Qatar 2022 fan festival, and at MDLBEAST and Mawsem Al Riyadh. In 2022 and 2023 he toured 14 US cities with DJ Aseel.

Off stage he builds tools for other producers, including the DJOW RVRS plugin and the Sharq sample packs, and runs the apparel label OWVRSZ.

Speaks Arabic, English and French.
