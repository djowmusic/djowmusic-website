Full

223 words

DJOW (Joseph Daou) is an international open format DJ and producer, born in Lebanon and based in Dubai.

He started in 2013 as the resident DJ of a small pub in Lebanon, playing the songs the whole room knew by heart. In 2022 he moved to Dubai, where crowds from every corner of the Arab world shaped his sound: Khaleeji rhythm, Oriental melody and Afro House drums in one set.

He is resident at Mood Rooftop and EPIK in Dubai and plays clubs, festivals and weddings across the Gulf, Lebanon, Egypt and Europe. He has played to 50,000+ at Expo 2020 Dubai and 30,000+ at the FIFA World Cup Qatar 2022 fan festival, with sets at MDLBEAST, Mawsem Al Riyadh and Beat the Heat Dubai. In 2022 and 2023 he toured 14 US cities with DJ Aseel.

His remixes give Arabic classics a club pulse, including his 2025 Afro remix of Fairuz's Btetloj El Deni. He has released more than 70 originals, remixes and mashups.

Off stage he builds tools for the next generation of DJs: DJOW RVRS, a reverse reverb plugin for Mac and Windows, DJOW SGNL, a free metering plugin and desktop gadget, and Sharq Vol. 1, a free sample pack of Oriental and Afro House sounds made with the premium oversized apparel label OWVRSZ. He speaks Arabic, English and French.
