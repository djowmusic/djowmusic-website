      Questions
      
Which areas do you cover?
Dubai, Abu Dhabi and the rest of the UAE, Lebanon, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain. Destination weddings abroad on request.
What music do you play at weddings?
Khaleeji, Oriental, Lebanese, Iraqi, Egyptian, Afro House and international hits. The playlist is built with the couple, including the songs that must play and the ones that must not.
Do you play the zaffe and the entrance?
Yes. The zaffe, the entrance, the first dance and the cake cutting are planned with the couple and cued with the planner and the venue on the day.
Do you bring sound and lighting?
Hotels usually provide the sound system and DJ setup through their AV team. Full sound and lighting can be arranged in Dubai when the venue does not provide it.
How far ahead should we book?
Peak season runs October to April. Book as soon as the venue and date are set, as weekend dates go first.
